About Nawa Mumbwe

Hi there! I'm Nawa Mumbwe, a passionate software engineer based in Northcliff, Johannesburg, South Africa. With over 5 years of experience in full-stack development, I specialize in building scalable web applications, modernizing legacy systems, and delivering cloud-based solutions.

Professional Journey​

My journey into software engineering began with a unique path - I hold a BSc in Geology from the University of Pretoria (2013-2017), which developed my analytical thinking and problem-solving skills. I then transitioned into tech through the WeThinkCode_ program in Johannesburg (2018-2019), where I completed the software engineering program 6 months ahead of schedule.
