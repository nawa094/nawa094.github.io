# About Me

Hi there! I'm **Nawa Mumbwe**, a software engineer based in South Africa with a passion for building modern web applications and sharing knowledge with the tech community.

## My Journey

I started my software engineering career in 2019 at an insurance company in Centurion, Pretoria. It was an incredible place to begin - filled with mentors who genuinely cared about helping me grow. Over the years, I've worked across different companies, including my current role at an investment bank in Sandton.

## What I Do

Currently, I work as a software engineer focusing on:

- **Frontend Development** with React, TypeScript, and modern web technologies
- **Backend Systems** using Node.js and various databases
- **Team Collaboration** and knowledge sharing
- **Mentoring** junior developers

## Why I Write

This blog is my way of giving back to the community that has taught me so much. I write about:

- **Technical tutorials** and lessons learned
- **Career insights** from the South African tech industry
- **Personal experiences** navigating workplace culture
- **Tools and productivity** tips for developers

## Beyond Code
